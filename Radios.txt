Part 95 Approved Handhelds (MURS/GMRS)
These radios are legally certified for Part 95 frequencies and are fully compatible with the Kenwood K1 mic plug.

1. BTECH MURS-V2
- Weather Resistance: Features an IP54 casing to withstand tough conditions and dust.
- Flexibility: Customize channels for MURS, NOAA weather, or scanner purposes.
- Price: ~$55

2. Wouxun KG-805M
- Capabilities: Full 2-watt transmit power across all 5 MURS channels.
- Durability: Built with an IP55 water-resistant rating for outdoor environments.
- Price: ~$100

3. BTECH GMRS-V2
- Display: Features a highly capable dual display with two independent push-to-talk buttons.
- Scanning: Monitor standard VHF and UHF channels while actively operating on GMRS.
- Price: ~$60

4. TERA TR-505
- Certifications: FCC Part 95 certified, capable of being programmed for either GMRS or MURS.
- Battery: Up to 30 hours of operation with the supplied 1600 mAh lithium-ion battery in power-saver mode, plus voice announcements for battery life.
- Price: ~$117

Non-Part 95 Handhelds (MURS Capable)
These are unlocked amateur/commercial radios that can physically tune to the 151-154 MHz MURS frequencies and utilize the Kenwood K1 plug.

1. Baofeng UV-5R
- Channels: Includes 128 programmable memory channels with VFO tuning.
- Compact Size: Very lightweight footprint, though it lacks dedicated water resistance.
- Price: ~$30

2. Baofeng UV-82
- Design: A taller, more robust form factor compared to standard entry-level models.
- Audio: Equipped with a notably louder and richer internal speaker and a dual PTT button.
- Price: ~$45

3. TYT TH-UV88
- Memory: Holds up to 200 channels for extensive scanning lists.
- Display: Features a high-contrast dot-matrix screen that is significantly easier to read.
- Price: ~$30

4. TERA TR-500
- Build Quality: Rugged, high-performance 16-channel Part 90 commercial transceiver.
- Battery: Operates 30+ hours between charges with voice announcements for battery life.
- Price: ~$130
